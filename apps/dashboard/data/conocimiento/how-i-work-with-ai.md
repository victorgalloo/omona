- Don't stall: whatever needs a person gets parked, and the rest keeps going.
  - Login, password, money or anything legal: logged as a blocker, with its link.
  - At the end, every blocker is delivered together.
- No micromanagement: if I have to check every step, the design is wrong.
- What cannot fail goes in code, not in the prompt.
  - The model writes. The code decides what may be said.
- “No data” is a valid answer. An invented number, never.
- When something goes up to a person, it goes as a yes-or-no question, with the link.
- Secrets live in a vault. No agent types passwords.
