- An agent brings it up to date every morning.
  - It moves yesterday into the journal and into each topic's note.
  - One note per topic, each with its dated history.
- Automatic capture: nobody has to say “write this down”.
  - Requests, decisions and changes flow in from the work with agents.
- What is left out
  - Personal life is not written down, not even redacted.
  - Private notes stay in the vault, for me only.
  - Passwords, keys, identity documents and health data: never.
- This page comes from there
  - Only notes I mark as public get out: an allowlist.
  - The exporter refuses if it sees phone numbers, third-party emails, amounts or internal links.
