- A four-step ladder
  - Email and domain: SPF, DMARC and a stop to fake invoices.
  - Website: HTTPS, HSTS and security headers.
  - Access and cloud: least privilege and secrets in a vault.
  - AI agents: defense against prompt injection and secure MCP servers.
- Defensive only: customer-owned systems, with written authorization.
  - No offensive pentest and no red team against third parties.
- New service: there are no customer engagements yet.
